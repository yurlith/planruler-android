PlanRuler is an offline-first Android tool for measuring PDF/PNG/JPEG plans,
calculating materials and workshop geometry, exporting results, and maintaining
an optional local CRM for installation work.

The upstream developer has authorised publication of the source and submission
to the official F-Droid repository. The app does not request Internet access
and contains no ads, analytics, tracking SDK, Firebase or Google Play Services.
It builds with Gradle, JDK 17 and Android SDK 36.

Proposed build metadata is available at
`fdroid/com.planruler.app.yml` in the source repository. Upstream Fastlane
metadata, icon and phone screenshots are included.
